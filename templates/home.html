How the examination works

  1. Register. Fill in the student and parent details and give parent or guardian consent. You receive a registration number immediately.
  2. Prepare. Each Olympiad publishes a syllabus overview, built around the school curriculum.
  3. Take the exam. Students sit a timed multiple-choice paper at their own class level.
  4. Check your status. Log in to see whether your registration is pending, confirmed or needs attention.
